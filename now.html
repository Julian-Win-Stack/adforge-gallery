Same links, one month apart

Left is what my app made on Oct 20. Right is what it made on Nov 18, one month later, from the exact same product links. Just the link, nothing else. Every grade is mine, by eye.

AI video still breaks sometimes, so the app is built around fixing it with a note. On Nov 18, 7 ads had a broken scene. For each one, I told the agent in the chat what was wrong, and it re-filmed only that scene. Under those ads you can see the 3 steps: the broken first try, my message, and the fixed scene.

Pass means I watched it and nothing broke: no wrong claim, no impossible motion, no made up product part. It doesn't mean the ad would sell.
